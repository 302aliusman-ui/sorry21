Pehla Raat

Pehli raat ko lagta tha thodi dair mein theek ho jayega. Phone uthaya — rakha. Screen dekha — band kiya. Har side mein tu nahi thi. Ye ahsaas pehli baar hua.

Doosra Din

Doosra din aaya — aur samajh aaya ye koi gham nahi, ye dard hai. Jiska koi ilaaj nahi. Har message delete kiya, har notification check kiya — bas tera naam dhundhta raha.

Teesra Din

Aaj teesra din hai. Ab dard ki jagah sukoon aa gaya hai — ek ajeeb sa sukoon. Jaise accept kar liya ki tu nahi aayegi. Lekin dil ke andar ek chhota sa hissa abhi bhi tere liye dhadakta hai.

Tumse Baat Hui

Agar meri kisi baat ne tumhe hurt kiya hai — ya meri kisi ghalti ne tumhara dil dukhaaya hai — toh main us ke liye maafi chahta hoon. Sirf maafi nahi, balki ek sachcha ahsaas-e-gunaah bhi.

Maine shayad wo nahi samjha jo tum chaahti thi, ya shayad samajh ke bhi nahi kar paaya. Ye meri kamzori hai, tumhari nahi. Tumne jo bhi feel kiya — wo bilkul sahi tha.

Main tumse koi umeed nahi rakh ke baitha — bas itna kehna chahta hoon ki tumhara jo bhi faisla ho, us mein main tum ko respect karunga. Tum meri koi zimmedari nahi ho... tum khud apni marzi ki malik ho.

Ye sirf ek mukatiba hai — ap se. Bina kisi shart ke, bina koi pressure ke.
